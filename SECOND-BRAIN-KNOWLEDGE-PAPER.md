# SECOND-BRAIN-KNOWLEDGE-PAPER.md — National Public Policy Analysis Advisor

A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

> **Note on sourcing:** This list was compiled from general subject-matter knowledge to give the skill a strong research foundation to build from. Before relying on any specific citation (e.g., in an academic, legal, or professional deliverable), verify the exact title, year, and venue independently, as this list has not been individually re-verified against live databases at compilation time.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. H. Lasswell (1956). The Decision Process: Seven Categories of Functional Analysis. University of Maryland Press — Foundational policy-cycle framework.
2. W. Jann, K. Wegrich (2007). Theories of the Policy Cycle. In Handbook of Public Policy Analysis — Modern policy-cycle model reference.
3. E. Bardach (2000). A Practical Guide for Policy Analysis: The Eightfold Path to More Effective Problem Solving. Chatham House — Foundational structured policy-alternatives methodology.
4. D. Weimer, A. Vining (2017). Policy Analysis: Concepts and Practice (6th ed.). Routledge — Standard comprehensive policy-analysis textbook.
5. J. Kingdon (1984). Agendas, Alternatives, and Public Policies. Little, Brown — Foundational agenda-setting theory (multiple-streams framework).
6. P. Sabatier, H. Jenkins-Smith (1993). Policy Change and Learning: An Advocacy Coalition Approach. Westview Press — Advocacy Coalition Framework for stakeholder-interest analysis.
7. T. Birkland (2019). An Introduction to the Policy Process (5th ed.). Routledge — Standard policy-process textbook covering stakeholder/evidence analysis.
8. M. Howlett, M. Ramesh, A. Perl (2009). Studying Public Policy: Policy Cycles and Policy Subsystems (3rd ed.). Oxford University Press — Comprehensive policy-cycle and subsystem analysis.
9. C. Freeman (1991). Networks of Innovators: A Synthesis of Research Issues. Research Policy — Stakeholder-network analysis methodology applicable to policy contexts.
10. OECD (2020). Policy Framework on Sound Public Governance. OECD Publishing — Applied cross-country evidence-based policy-governance framework.
11. R. Haynes, A. Hare (2003). Framework for the Application of Systematic Review Methodology to the Field of Legislation. Cochrane Collaboration — Evidence-synthesis methodology applicable to policy evaluation.
12. D. Stone (2012). Policy Paradox: The Art of Political Decision Making (3rd ed.). Norton — Framework emphasizing multiple, contested framings of policy problems.
13. A. Wildavsky (1979). Speaking Truth to Power: The Art and Craft of Policy Analysis. Little, Brown — Classic reference on the analytical craft of policy work.
14. P. May (1991). Reconsidering Policy Design: Policies and Publics. Journal of Public Policy — Policy-design theory relevant to alternatives evaluation.
15. C. Lindblom (1959). The Science of 'Muddling Through'. Public Administration Review — Foundational incrementalist policy-process theory (alternative to rational comprehensive models).
16. World Bank (2016). World Development Report: Governance and the Law. World Bank — Cross-country empirical governance and policy-implementation evidence base.
17. J. Pressman, A. Wildavsky (1973). Implementation. University of California Press — Foundational policy-implementation-stage analysis.
18. E. Ostrom (1990). Governing the Commons: The Evolution of Institutions for Collective Action. Cambridge University Press — Institutional-analysis framework relevant to policy-design evaluation.
19. UNDP (2009). Users' Guide to Measuring Public Administration Performance. United Nations Development Programme — Applied cross-country policy-evaluation methodology.
20. A. Boaz, J. Davies, A. Fraser, S. Nutley (eds.) (2019). What Works Now? Evidence-Informed Policy and Practice. Policy Press — Modern evidence-based policy-analysis reference.
